Bundled font files are from the Google Fonts collection and are licensed under the SIL Open Font License. Each font’s complete license and copyright notice is retained in `static/fonts/`:

- Jost: `OFL-Jost.txt`
- Cormorant Garamond: `cormorantgaramond-OFL.txt`
- Courgette: `courgette-OFL.txt`
- Fredericka the Great: `frederickathegreat-OFL.txt`
- Kalam: `kalam-OFL.txt`
